Introduction

Reason for this plan

Plan Purpose

  • Mission: Deliver efficient service, delight customers with timely solutions.
  • Vision: Empower future operations; innovate help desk efficiency endlessly.

SMART Goals & Benefits

  • Goal 1: Increase ticket resolution by 20% before 2026.
    Benefit: Faster support response enhances productivity.

  • Goal 2: Reduce average response time by 20% before January 2026.
    Benefit: Quick replies boost operational efficiency.

  • Goal 3: Increase positive feedback rate by 90% before June 2025. Benefit: Improved service quality to ensure customer satisfaction.

  • Goal 4:Decrease waiting times by 50% before April 2026.
    Benefit: To reduce waiting times.

  • Goal 5: Increase funds for training staff by 50% before December 2025.
    Benefit: Skilled staff improve user satisfaction.
